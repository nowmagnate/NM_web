## What changed, briefly

The old system was a light editorial studio look: cool off-white canvas, a
cobalt accent, Cabinet Grotesk plus Geist plus Geist Mono, a double-bezel card
construction, and section eyebrows.

It was replaced wholesale rather than polished, because the request was a
redesign and the old look was treated as evidence rather than authority. Three
of its devices are now explicitly banned in the new system:

- **Eyebrows** (the small uppercase kicker above a heading) — banned outright.
- **A mono face used for micro-labels** — monospace as a costume for
  "technical" is a category tell; there is no mono face in the new system.
- **Cards as page structure** — the capability grid is a milled index now.

The asset manifest beside this file ([`ASSET-MANIFEST.md`](./ASSET-MANIFEST.md))
is still current: every image slot and its dimensions are unchanged by the
redesign, and all of them are still placeholders.
